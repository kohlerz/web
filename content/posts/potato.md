---
title: Automated Optical Produce Grader
date: 2025-09-01
---

This project was an automated optical produce grader for a local potato farm in Wisconsin. The goal of the project was to assist in finding defect potatoes as they were traveling down the production line and automatically track and kick them off the line for separate processing. The system used industrial machine-vision cameras to send high volume data back to a computer running our custom software.

We used various types of machine-vision algorithms and machine-leaning models to detect the defects on the potatoes. Given a large amount of training data from cameras placed above the line, we were able to create custom training datasets and use them to train our models on powerful local hardware. Once a defect was found we needed to track the potato across the camera and kick it as it was falling through the air. I was lucky enough to be able to work with a brilliant team of engineers to bring this project from an idea into reality and it is currently in production. The project resulted in significant improvement in produce quality as shown by a third party customer audit.
