### Team 171 - Cheese Curd Herd

I got started in robotics with my local FIRST Robotics team, FRC Team 171 - The Cheese Curd Herd. It was a joint effort between Platteville High School students and UW-Platteville students who were our mentors. This is where I got my first experience with CAD, 3D printing, machining, programming, and electronics. Once I my first real experience with engineering and bringing a system up from zero, I was hooked. The team was very close-knit and I was able to make some great friends and memories.

The FRC season is a 6 week period where teams are given a new challenge and must design, build, and program a robot to compete in the challenge. At the end of the 6 weeks, we had to put our robot in a locked bag and could no longer modify it until competition. During season I would spend upwards of 40 hours a week working on the robot. Once competition season came around, we would spend our weekends traveling to different events to compete with our robot against other teams from around the midwest.

One of the many projects I worked on during the off-season was a demonstration robot that we could take to local schools and outreach events to show what FIRST Robotics was all about. It had a mechanum drivetrain which allowed it to move in any direction. As part of the demonstration, I wrote a program that showed which direction each wheel was spinning on an addressable LED strip that was mounted on the side of the robot. It used serial communication with the roboRIO (FRC main control board) to read the values being output to each wheel, and light up the corresponding leds with the speed. It helped me demonstrate how when the mechanum wheels turned opposite each other, the robot would strafe side to side.
