### Wisconsin Robotics - 2021-2025

I was heavily involved in Wisconsin Robotics starting my first semester at UW-Madison. I started on the electrical team and joined a small team that remained after COVID-19. I had to get myself acquainted with the team and its practices very quickly, and there was no remaining electrical engineer to mentor me. I had already learned Autodesk EAGLE, so I was able to look at some of the designs of the boards that the graduated students had made for the rover as it stood.

![WReaper PCB](/WR/IMG_0088.jpeg)

Using the references I could gather, I designed my first PCB for the rover called WReaper2. It was the motherboard designed to hold the IMU, GPS, Raspberry Pi, and a logic level converter for serial communication between the Pi and the motor controllers. It also held some solid state relays to control auxiliary devices a solenoid used to type on a keyboard.

![WReaper V2](/WR/IMG_0164.jpeg)

I became more heavily involved in the team as the years went on and it grew in size. I became president for the 2024-2025 season, and that year we felt confident enough to do a full redesign of the rover for the first time in five years. I took more of a leadership role in the electrical team and started to be more of a mentor to the other members. I influenced the design of the new rover heavily and assisted for the more complex PCBs. I also did PCB reviews to ensure that the designs were sound and that the PCBs would work as intended.

![New Drive PCB System](/WR/IMG_2522.JPG)

With the new year I designed a large portion of the swerve mechanism of the rover, as well as the control system for the drive and arm. We used open source VESC controllers for the drive with the idea of expanding our capabilities into custom motor controllers in the future.

![Team Photo](/WR/IMG_2883.PNG)


There is so much more I could say about my time on Wisconsin Robotics, but I'll leave that for another time. I had such a wonderful time and I am so grateful for the experiences I had and the people I met along the way.
