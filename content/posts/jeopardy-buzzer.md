---
title: Custom Jeopardy Buzzer
date: 2021-04-26
---

My physics professor propositioned me and a friend to make a Jeopardy Buzzer style device that could be reliable enough and easy to use for my high-school's pep rallies and assemblies.

I had recently been working more and more with my 3D printer, so I took on the challenge and designed the mechanical and electrical aspects of the device. I had experience with CAD from working on the robotics team so I designed the parts in Fusion 360.

Each baton is made from clear PETG with addressable LED strips embedded into the casing. I used ethernet jacks to send data and power to each buzzer. The ethernet jacks all connected to the main hub which contained an arduino to do all the processing. The device was simple to use and started the sequence as soon as power was applied, with four distinct lighting patterns and a reset port for the announcer to be able to reset the system. The system was fully functional and was play tested at the high school successfully.
